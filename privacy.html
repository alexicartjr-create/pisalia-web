Pisalia privacy policy

Version 1.7, of 7 November 2026.

This is the privacy policy of the Pisalia app (“Pisalia: flatmates”), distributed on Google Play (Android) and the App Store (iPhone). It is the same one you can read inside the app, in Profile → Privacy.

This is a translation of the Spanish original. If there is any doubt or discrepancy between the two, the Spanish version prevails.

Privacy contact: info@pisalia.app

Deleting your account and your data: how to do it, with or without the app.

Data controller

Alex Icart Ramiro. Contact: info@pisalia.app. More details in Provider details.

What data we handle

The data you give us: email, name, age, occupation, your profile photo, the description about you, town and search radius, test answers, listings with their photos (and, if you add it, the exact address of the flat, which stays on your phone like everything else), requests, messages, viewings, reviews and reports (which may include someone else’s data), and what you note down in “Search together”: budget, workplace, flats and notes. From your gallery or camera we only use the photos you choose: your profile photo, your listing photos and, if you do “Photo checked”, the selfie you take for it (in the beta it doesn’t leave your phone). We don’t access your phone’s location or your contacts. On Android, “Take a photo” opens the camera inside Pisalia (on iPhone, only in “Photo checked”; for your photo and your listings, the iPhone uses its own picker): the first time, your phone asks for permission; it is only used while that screen is open, without recording video or sound, and photos aren’t saved to your phone’s gallery or sent to anyone.

What for

To create your account and give you access, show you profiles you have affinity with and listings that meet your preferences, let users contact each other, prevent scams and abuse (warnings in the chat, offensive language filter, reports and blocks) and, if you ask, check that your profile photo is of you (“Photo checked”, simulated in the beta). We don’t sell your data or use it for advertising.

Legal basis

Providing the service you ask for when you sign up (art. 6.1.b of the GDPR) and the legitimate interest in keeping the community safe (art. 6.1.f), which is also the basis for your profile photo: it helps you know who you are talking to and curbs fake profiles. In the beta, “Photo checked” is a simulation: the selfie doesn’t leave your phone and nobody reviews it.

Your right to object

At any time you can object to what we do on the basis of legitimate interest. Write to us at info@pisalia.app and we will stop, unless there are compelling legitimate grounds that carry more weight (for example, keeping the evidence of a report that protects someone else); in that case we will explain why.

Required data

To create your account we need your email address, your name and, if you are looking, your age and your town: without them you can’t sign up. The profile photo is needed to contact people and to post, not to browse. To post a listing you need the title, the town, the price, the number of bedrooms and 3 photos. Everything else is optional: if you don’t give it, it shows as “Not specified” or whatever depends on it isn’t worked out (without the test there is no affinity). The smoking question in the test is optional and asks what you prefer for the home, not whether you smoke.

Phone permissions

They are all optional: the camera, to take your photos with “Take a photo”; photos, only the ones you choose with your phone’s picker (we don’t ask for access to your gallery); and Face ID, your fingerprint or your phone’s passcode, only if you turn on the app lock. Today we don’t ask for notifications. If you refuse a permission, the app works without that feature, and you can withdraw them in your phone’s settings.

If you write to us

Emails arrive in our info@pisalia.app mailbox, provided by DonDominio on servers in Spain, which handles them on our behalf and under contract. Never send us your DNI (Spanish ID card), passwords or codes.

Where it is stored

In the beta, only on your phone and, if you have it turned on, in your Google or iCloud account backup. Pisalia has no servers: nobody else sees your data, except what you decide to send or share.

What other people see

Your name and profile photo, your age, occupation and town, the description about you, your test answers and affinity, your living style (worked out from your test), your approximate budget, the move-in date you’re looking for and its flexibility (if you add it), your checks and reviews, and your listings. We never show your email or your mobile number. In the beta nobody sees them: the other profiles are examples.

Profile photo

You need it to send or accept requests, write in the chat and post listings, as a tenant and as a landlord: it helps you know who you are talking to before you meet. Without it you can browse profiles and listings. It has to be a real photo of you, with your face visible. Other people see it on your profile, in your requests and chats, in your listings and in “Search together”. We don’t use it for facial recognition or to verify your identity. In the beta it is only stored on your phone and nobody else sees it: the other profiles are examples. You can change it whenever you like from your profile. It can’t be removed while you have the account and it is deleted when you delete the account.

Photo checked

Only if you ask for it. In the beta it is a simulation: you take a selfie with the gesture the app asks for, but it doesn’t leave your phone, nobody reviews it and it is deleted when you finish or cancel; we only store that you have it (simulated) and the date. With “Take the selfie”, the camera inside Pisalia doesn’t leave any copy. Only if Pisalia can’t use the camera and you choose your phone’s camera app, on Android that app also leaves a temporary copy in a Pisalia folder, which Pisalia deletes a few seconds later or, at the latest, when you leave the app or open it again (on Android 10 or earlier, while it exists, other apps with permission to read storage could see it). When Pisalia has a server, a person on the moderation team will compare the selfie with your profile photo by eye. Pisalia doesn’t verify identity documents: we won’t ask you for your DNI, NIE (foreigner identity number) or passport.

Session and app lock

The session stays open on your phone so you don’t have to enter a code every time. After 30 days without opening the app, the session closes by itself: to know this, the app notes on your phone when you last used it, and doesn’t send it to us. If you turn on the lock (with Face ID, Touch ID, your fingerprint or your face, or with your phone’s passcode, PIN or pattern), the app covers itself when you leave it and asks you to unlock it when you open it and when you come back after more than a minute. Your phone checks it: the app only knows whether it worked or not, and we never receive or keep your face, your fingerprint or that code.

Third parties

Map: the Instituto Geográfico Nacional (Spain’s national mapping agency, ign.es) serves the images and receives your IP address and the area you are looking at. The map and PDF libraries are built into the app: they aren’t downloaded from any server. Google (on Google Play) or Apple (on the App Store) distributes the app and handles the download data under its own policy. If you open a route or a search in Google Maps or Apple Maps, a search on Idealista, Fotocasa or Habitaclia, a listing link or your email, you leave Pisalia with the data in the link (for example, an address or an area) and that service’s policy applies. What you copy or share, such as a PDF, you send yourself to whoever you choose.

How long

Until you delete your account from your profile or delete the app.

If you write to us (a query, a report you send us or a request about your data), we keep your emails for 12 months from when the matter is closed, and requests about your data with our reply for 3 years. After that we delete them.

Your rights

You can access your data, rectify it, erase it, object to or restrict its use, ask for portability and withdraw your consent by writing to info@pisalia.app. In the beta you can also do it yourself: change your data from your profile and delete it with “Delete my account”. If you aren’t happy with how we deal with it, you can complain to the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (AEPD, Spain’s data protection authority, aepd.es).

Under-18s

Pisalia is only for people aged 18 or over. We don’t knowingly handle data of under-18s. If we have signs that an account belongs to someone under 18, we may suspend it while we check.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will tell you in the app.
