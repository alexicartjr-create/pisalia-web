Pisalia

The app for finding a room and flatmates who fit the way you live. In Spanish, Catalan and English.

Pisalia is in beta. The other profiles and listings are examples, the checks are simulated and your data is only stored on your phone: Pisalia doesn’t have servers yet. Only for over-18s.
