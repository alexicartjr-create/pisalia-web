How it works, in 3 steps

  1. Choose what you’re looking for. A room, people to share a flat with, someone for the spare room in your flat, or tenants for your flat.
  2. Take the living-style test. 12 questions and an optional one about schedules, tidiness, cleaning, guests, noise, pets or budget. Mark what really matters to you and what you don’t mind.
  3. See your affinity and get in touch. Each person shows your affinity and what you have in common, and each listing shows how many of your preferences it meets. Send a request: if they accept, it’s a match and the chat opens.

For people looking and people letting

If you’re looking for a room or flatmates

  • People looking for a flat near you, with your affinity and what you have in common.
  • Rooms in a list or on the map, with filters for requirements and preferences.
  • The real move-in cost of each listing: rent, bills, deposit and estimated upfront cost. Anything missing shows as “Not specified”.
  • Viewings: pick one of the times offered by whoever posted the listing, or suggest another.
  • Search together: with someone you’ve matched with, look for a flat with your budget and areas, vote on flats, comment on them and send a joint request.

If you’re letting a room or a flat

  • Post your room or flat with photos. Pause it, renew it or mark it as let.
  • See each candidate with their affinity and the requirements they meet, and accept or decline them.
  • Schedule: offer the times when the flat can be viewed and organise the viewings.
  • When you post, the app won’t let you ask for what the law doesn’t allow tenants to be charged, such as agency fees.

Safety and trust

  • Scam warnings in the chat

    The chat warns you if a message looks like a typical scam, such as asking for money before the viewing, or if you’re about to send an ID card, an IBAN or a card number.

  • Safety checklist before you pay

    Six checks you tick yourself before paying anything, with a tip for each one.

  • Report and block

    From any profile, listing or chat, without telling the other person. In the beta, reports stay on your phone and nobody reviews them yet: if you want us to look at one, you can email it to us.

  • Listing review

    When Pisalia has a server, a person will review every listing before it’s published. In the beta, the listings you see are examples and yours stay on your phone.

  • Pisalia doesn’t take or handle payments

    No rent, no deposits, no holding deposits. Don’t pay anything until you’ve seen the flat and signed the contract.

  • Your number stays yours

    You talk in the Pisalia chat and arrange the viewing without giving out your phone number.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost?

Nothing. Pisalia is free and has no advertising.

When can I download it?

Pisalia is coming to Android first, on Google Play. We’ll soon open a closed test: if you have an Android phone, are over 18 and live in Spain, email us at info@pisalia.app and we’ll let you know when you can try it. The iPhone version will come later.

Where does it work?

Pisalia is for the whole of Spain, but we’re starting in Catalonia. In the beta, the sample profiles and listings are in the province of Barcelona.

What is affinity?

A percentage that compares your answers to the living-style test and takes into account what each person marks as really important. It explains what you have in common and where you differ. It’s a guide, not a guarantee.

What is Search together?

If you’ve matched with someone and want to share a flat, you can look together: with your budget, areas and dates, you save flats, vote on them, comment on them and send a joint request to whoever posted the listing.

Does Pisalia take payments or manage the rental?

No. Pisalia only puts people in touch: it doesn’t take or handle rent, deposits or holding deposits, and it isn’t a party to the contract.

Who can use it?

Only over-18s. It’s designed mainly for young people who study or work and are looking for a room or flatmates.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Profile → Delete my account. If you no longer have the app, you can ask us by email: we explain how in Delete your account.

More questions? See support or email us at info@pisalia.app.

Want to try it before anyone else?

Email us at info@pisalia.app from your Google account’s email address and we’ll let you know when the Android closed test opens.
