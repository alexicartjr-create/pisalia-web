Pisalia support

Having a problem or a question with the app? Here’s how to contact us and the answers to the most frequently asked questions.

Emergencies

If you are in danger or something serious has happened, call 112. It’s free and works from any phone. Pisalia isn’t an emergency service.

Contact

Write to us at info@pisalia.app. We can help you in Spanish, Catalan and English.

Tell us what happened and which phone you use (Android or iPhone) and, if you can, add a screenshot. Never send us codes, passwords or your DNI (Spanish ID card).

We keep your emails for 12 months from when the matter is closed, and requests about your data with our reply for 3 years. After that we delete them. Pisalia’s mailbox is provided by DonDominio, on servers in Spain, and handles emails on our behalf and under contract. More details in the privacy policy.

Pisalia is published by a private individual. Their details are in the provider details.

Frequently asked questions

I’m not getting the code to log in

Pisalia is in beta and doesn’t send emails or text messages yet. The 6-digit code appears on the same screen where you have to enter it, in the box “Beta: we don’t send emails. Your test code is…” (or “Beta: we don’t send texts…” when you verify your mobile). Tap Use so you don’t have to type it.

  • Each code expires after 10 minutes and, if you get it wrong 5 times, it stops working. Then tap Resend code (or Get another code, if you are verifying your mobile) and you’ll get a new one. If the app asks you to wait, wait a few seconds and tap it again.
  • Pisalia will never ask you for a code. If someone asks you for it, don’t give it to them.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Profile → Delete my account. If you no longer have the app, you can ask us by email. All the steps are in Delete your Pisalia account.

How do I report or block someone?

From their profile (Report profile), from the listing (Report listing) or from the chat (the three-dot button, “More options” → Report). Choose the reason, add details if you like and tap Save report. We don’t tell the other person. Afterwards you can block them: you won’t see their profile or their messages again.

In the beta, reports are only saved on your phone and nobody reviews them yet. If you want us to look at one, tap Email it too when you save it, or write to us.

Where is my data?

In the beta, only on your phone (and in your Google or iCloud account backup, if you have it turned on). Pisalia has no servers. More details in the privacy policy.

How do I change the language?

In Profile → Language (Castellano, Català or English), or on the first screen with “Ver en castellano”, “Veure en català” or “View in English”.

Safety tips against scams

These are the safety tips in the app (Profile → Safety tips). They help you avoid most rental scams.

If you’re looking for a room

  • View before you pay

    Don’t pay a holding deposit, a booking fee or the deposit before you’ve seen the flat in person and signed the contract.

  • Check who’s renting it out

    Ask for the landlord’s DNI (Spanish ID card) and a nota simple from the Registro de la Propiedad (Land Registry), which you can get at registradores.org for a few euros, to confirm the flat is theirs.

  • Pay in a traceable way

    By bank transfer to an account in the landlord’s name, with a receipt. Never by Western Union, gift cards or crypto.

  • Don’t be rushed

    “Lots of people are interested, pay today to secure it” is the classic scam line.

  • Price too good to be true

    If it’s well below prices in the area, be extra careful. Pisalia warns you when it spots one.

  • Your documents, last

    Don’t send a photo of your DNI, payslips or bank details until you’ve viewed the flat and have the contract in front of you.

  • Talk on Pisalia

    Stick to the Pisalia chat until you’ve met the person: there we warn you about signs of a scam, and you can report or block them.

  • Never share codes

    If someone asks you for a code you got by text or email, they’re trying to get into your account. Don’t give it to them.

  • Be smart about viewings

    Go during the day, tell someone where you are and, if you can, take someone with you. Use “Tell someone” for your upcoming viewings.

  • During the viewing

    Don’t hand over documents or money. Check that whoever shows you the flat has the key and that their name matches the one on the listing.

If you’re letting a flat or a room

  • Meet the candidate

    Do the viewing in person or by video call, and look at their affinity, checks and reviews.

  • Check payments in your bank account

    Fake payment receipts are common. Don’t hand over the keys until you see the money in your account.

  • Watch out for overpayments

    If someone says they’ve sent you too much and asks you to pay back the difference, it’s a scam.

  • Contract and deposit

    Always sign a written contract and lodge the deposit with the official body in your autonomous community (region).

  • Look after candidates’ data

    Only ask for the documents you need, keep them safe and delete them once they’re no longer needed.

  • Talk on Pisalia

    Stick to the Pisalia chat until you’ve met the person: there we warn you about signs of a scam, and you can report or block them.

  • Never share codes

    If someone asks you for a code you got by text or email, they’re trying to get into your account. Don’t give it to them.

If something doesn’t add up, report it from the profile, the listing or the chat. We don’t tell the other person.

If you’ve already paid or given your details to a scammer, keep the screenshots and receipts, tell your bank as soon as possible and report it to the Policía Nacional, the Guardia Civil or, in Catalonia, the Mossos d’Esquadra.
