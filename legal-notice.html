Why there’s no tax ID (NIF) or address
Pisalia is a free service offered by a private individual, with no advertising, sponsorship, donations or any other income. So it isn’t an economic activity for the purposes of Ley 34/2002, Spain’s information society services act (as interpreted by the AEPD, Spain’s data protection authority, in E/03379/2021). If one day it becomes one, we will also publish the NIF and the address here.
What Pisalia is
A platform for finding flatmates and renting rooms or flats. Pisalia only puts people in touch: it isn’t a party to rental contracts.
Point of contact
For users (art. 12 of the EU Digital Services Act) and for the authorities of EU Member States, the European Commission and the European Board for Digital Services (art. 11): info@pisalia.app. A person deals with it, in Spanish, Catalan and English.
Reporting content
From any profile, listing or chat. In the beta, reports are only saved on your phone. If you want us to look at one, you can email it to us when you save it.
