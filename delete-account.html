Delete your Pisalia account

Here we explain how to delete your Pisalia account (“Pisalia: flatmates”, for Android and iPhone) and what happens to your data. Pisalia is developed and published by a private individual (their details are in the provider details).

Pisalia is in beta and has no servers. Your account and your data are only stored on your phone, so the quickest way to delete everything is from the app or by uninstalling it.

From the app (immediately)

  1. Open Pisalia and go to the Profile tab (bottom right).
  2. Scroll down to the Account section and tap Delete my account.
  3. Pisalia asks you to confirm: tap the red button to delete the account (or Cancel if you change your mind).
  4. You’ll see “Account and data deleted” and Pisalia goes back to the first screen.

It works the same whether you use Pisalia as a tenant or as a landlord. If you have the app in Spanish, the buttons are called Perfil → Eliminar mi cuenta; in Catalan, Perfil → Elimina el meu compte. If you logged out, log in again with your email to see the Profile tab, or uninstall the app.

By uninstalling the app

When you uninstall Pisalia, your phone also deletes all its data: account, profile, listings, photos and messages.

  • Android: press and hold the Pisalia icon and choose “Uninstall” (or go to Settings → Apps → Pisalia → Uninstall).
  • iPhone: press and hold the Pisalia icon, choose “Remove App”, then “Delete App” and confirm with “Delete”.

Without the app: ask us by email

If you no longer have Pisalia installed or can’t open it, write to us at info@pisalia.app.

  • Subject: “Delete my Pisalia account”.
  • Tell us the email address you signed up to Pisalia with and your profile name.
  • If you can, write to us from that same email address: that way we know the account is yours.
  • We don’t need your DNI (Spanish ID card), any password or any code. Don’t send them to us.

As Pisalia has no servers in the beta, we don’t keep your account or the app’s data outside your phone and we can’t delete them remotely: what is on the phone is deleted from the app or by uninstalling it. We’ll reply to confirm this and we’ll delete the emails you have sent us (for example, queries or reports).

What data is deleted

Everything Pisalia stores on your phone:

  • your account (the email address you log in with);
  • your profile: profile photo, name, age, occupation, the description about you, town and search radius, and your checks;
  • your living-style test answers;
  • your listings and their photos, including drafts;
  • requests, messages, viewings, reviews, reports and blocks;
  • what you noted down in “Search together”: budget, workplace, flats and notes.

What is kept and for how long

  • The app’s language (Spanish, Catalan or English), so we can keep showing you Pisalia in your language. It stays on the phone until you uninstall the app.
  • Backups. If you have your Google or iCloud account backup turned on, it may keep Pisalia data from before you deleted the account. That backup is managed by Google or Apple, not Pisalia: it is usually replaced by your phone’s next backup, and you can delete it yourself from the backup settings of your Google or iCloud account.
  • The emails you have sent us (queries, reports you sent with “Email it too” or a deletion request) are kept in Pisalia’s mailbox, provided by DonDominio on servers in Spain. Deleting the account in the app or uninstalling it doesn’t delete them sooner. We keep your emails for 12 months from when the matter is closed, and requests about your data with our reply for 3 years. After that we delete them. We keep requests about your data (for example, a request to delete your account) so that we can show we dealt with them.
  • What you sent outside Pisalia (emails, PDFs or texts you copied or shared) is held by the people or services you sent it to. Pisalia can’t delete it.
  • The download data for the app is handled by Google (on Google Play) or Apple (on the App Store) under its own policy.

Apart from those emails, Pisalia doesn’t keep any of your data: in the beta it has no servers.

Time limits

  • From the app or by uninstalling it: deletion is immediate.
  • By email: we reply within one month at most from when we receive your request, which is the time limit set by the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). If the request is complex, this can be extended by two more months; if that happens, we’ll tell you within the first month.

If you aren’t happy with how we deal with it, you can complain to the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (AEPD, Spain’s data protection authority, aepd.es). More information in the privacy policy.
