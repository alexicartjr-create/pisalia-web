Pisalia terms of use and rules

Version 1.7, of 7 November 2026.

They are the same ones you accept when you sign up in the app. Inside Pisalia they are in Profile → Terms of use and Profile → Community rules.

This is a translation of the Spanish original. If there is any doubt or discrepancy between the two, the Spanish version prevails.

Terms of use

  • What Pisalia is

    A platform for finding flatmates and renting rooms or flats. Pisalia isn’t a party to the rental contracts you sign.

  • It only puts people in touch

    Pisalia takes no part in negotiating or signing the contract, which happen outside the app, and it doesn’t offer bookings, payments or contract signing.

  • Automatic checks, no review

    In the beta, your listings are only on your phone and nobody reviews them: the app only checks a few things when you save them (contact details, offensive or discriminatory words and what the law doesn’t allow you to ask for). We also don’t check that the flat exists, who owns it, that the advertiser has permission to rent it out or that the details are true. If damage is caused by us breaking these terms or the law, we are liable for it.

  • What the badges mean

    “Email confirmed” and “Photo checked” (a selfie that a person on our team compares with the profile photo) don’t say who anyone is or guarantee that they can be trusted. Pisalia doesn’t verify identity documents. In the beta they are simulated.

  • Listings within the law

    Under the housing rules, you can’t charge the tenant agency fees or management costs, ask for more than one month’s deposit or additional guarantees of more than two months (one for a fixed-term (seasonal) contract), require rent-default insurance or more than one month in advance: the app won’t let you post it. In Catalonia, in a rent-pressure area (zona tensionada), the listing states the maximum price under the reference index. Anyone who sublets needs their landlord’s written permission. You can ask people to show they can pay, with any stable income or a guarantor, but you can’t require a single document.

  • Who to live with

    Only someone who lives in the flat can state a sex preference for flatmates (for example, a girls-only flat). A landlord or agency who doesn’t live there, or the listing for a whole flat, can’t, but they can say who lives there now. The age filter in People only exists among people looking for someone to live with: it can’t be used to choose tenants.

  • Who provides the service

    Alex Icart Ramiro. Their details and contact are in Provider details.

  • Who can use it

    People aged 18 or over, using real details.

  • Your account

    You are responsible for what is done with your account, unless it is due to a security failure on our part. Protect your email and never share the code.

  • Your content

    You are responsible for what you post and for it following the community rules.

  • Zero tolerance

    You can’t post or send offensive, sexual, violent, discriminatory or fraudulent content, or harass or threaten anyone. Pisalia blocks offensive language before it is posted.

  • Reports and blocks

    You can report and block from any profile, listing and chat. In the beta, reports are only saved on your phone and nobody reviews them yet. If you want us to look at one, you can email it to us when you save it.

  • Payments

    Pisalia doesn’t charge or handle rent, deposits or holding deposits.

  • Closing your account

    You can delete your account whenever you like from your profile.

  • Changes

    We’ll let you know if these terms change.

Community rules

So Pisalia is a safe place for everyone.

  • Be yourself

    Real profile and photos. Don’t pretend to be anyone else.

  • Respect

    No insults, harassment or discrimination.

  • No accommodation in exchange for sex

    Offering accommodation in exchange for sex, favours or affection, or asking for intimate photos, isn’t allowed on Pisalia.

  • View before you pay

    Don’t ask for or pay holding deposits before seeing the flat and signing the contract.

  • Honest listings

    Price, bills and conditions clear from the start.

  • 18+ only

    Pisalia isn’t for under-18s.

  • Report anything that doesn’t add up

    Every profile, listing and chat has an option to report or block.

Zero tolerance: you can’t post offensive, sexual, violent or discriminatory content, and anyone who harasses someone or posts that kind of content will have their account closed.
